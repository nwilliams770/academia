At 0° the two blue legs form one straight line and the joint is their midpoint. That collinearity IS the C¹ condition — and it's what a pen tool enforces when one handle swings to mirror the other.

C² — curvature

0

—

Drag it: C¹ stays true, C² goes false, and nothing visibly changes. No corner appears. That's the honest answer to "why would I need C²" — you don't, for silhouette. You need it for reflections off a surface and for motion without jerk.
