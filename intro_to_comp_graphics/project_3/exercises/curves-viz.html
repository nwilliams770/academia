Basis Functions

b₀(t) = (1−t)³  → P₀
b₁(t) = 3(1−t)²t  → P₁
b₂(t) = 3(1−t)t²  → P₂
b₃(t) = t³  → P₃
At every t the four heights sum to 1. Note b₁ and b₂ are zero at both ends — that's why the curve never touches P₁ and P₂.
